# Single-agent baseline

In one pass, critically assess the idea, design the smallest useful solution,
and verify your recommendation against the original constraints and evidence.
Compare a manual or deterministic baseline; distinguish feasibility from value.
Identify assumptions, unsupported claims, risks and one smallest next experiment.
Use continue, revise or stop as the decision. Continue means test, not validated.
Do not invent external facts or sources. Label claims using the supplied IDs.
Follow the caller's JSON contract and write concise Chinese.
